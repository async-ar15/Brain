# 30. Final Interview Simulation - Pre-requisites

## The Night Before

1. **Stop studying.** Trying to cram a new DP algorithm 12 hours before your interview will only cause anxiety and cognitive fatigue.
2. **Review your mistakes.** Read through the `revise.md` sheets for the topics you struggled with the most. Don't look at the code, just read "The Bridge" and "Optimized Intuition" sections.
3. **Get sleep.** A rested brain that can communicate clearly is worth more than a tired brain that memorized one extra LeetCode Hard problem.

## The Environment Setup

If your interview is remote:
- Have a glass of water nearby.
- Ensure your internet connection is stable.
- Close all unnecessary tabs and applications (especially Discord/Slack to avoid notification sounds).
- Have a pen and paper ready. Sometimes drawing a tree or a matrix by hand is faster than trying to visualize it in your head.
